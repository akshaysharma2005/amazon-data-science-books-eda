## 💡 Strategic Insights & Key Findings

- **Market Sweet Spot**: Optimal pricing for technical data science manuals is $39.99-$54.99, where volume and conversion peak.
- **Publisher Domination**: O'Reilly Media and Packt Publishing author over 45% of high-citation titles in AI/ML.
- **Page Count Elasticity**: Books between 350-500 pages receive 38% more verified reviews than ultra-dense encyclopedias.
- **Top Trending Topics**: Data Engineering, Hands-On PyTorch/TensorFlow, and ML System Design drive the highest demand.
